Hello everyone!

A lifetime devoted
to the Italian language.

I’m Adriana Meloni, and I’ve taught Italian language and literature for more than 40 years, especially in Israel. I began teaching in France before earning my degree in Modern Literature at the University of Strasbourg in 1985, with a thesis on the Italian and French Renaissance.

From 1984 to 1986, I taught languages in France, including at the Strasbourg Chamber of Commerce. Later, in Milan, I taught French language and literature at Sally Mayer High School from 1988 to 1990, and at private schools until 1992.

From 1992 to 2000, I worked as an editorial reader for Bompiani and Mondadori. I also translated two art books from Italian into French and wrote editorial notes for Bompiani and Mondadori on a book by Elie Wiesel.

After moving to Israel in 2000, I taught at the Open University, the Italian Cultural Institute in Tel Aviv, and the institution now known as Reichman University in Herzliya. I also gave lectures on Italian literature, and since 2012 I’ve prepared students for the Israeli Italian matriculation exam (Bagrut).

In 2013, I followed the master’s course in teaching Italian as a second language (L2) at the University for Foreigners of Perugia, completing all the coursework examinations and assessments without receiving the master’s degree. In 2020, I undertook training at the University of Siena in teaching Italian and preparing students for the B1 exam required for Italian citizenship. Since 2021, I’ve focused on personalized private lessons.
